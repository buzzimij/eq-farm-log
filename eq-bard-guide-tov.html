Haste & DPS Testing
Key Findings — Empirically Tested on Frostreaver
Winner
Verses of Victory beats McVaxius for DPS. VoV 30% haste vs McVaxius 22% = 103 more hits over 30 min. +9% DPS. McVaxius ATK+14 only adds ~+2 avg damage/hit — nowhere near enough to compensate.
Confirmed
Brass horn has zero impact on swing speed. Tested: 36.0 swings/min with and without horn. Instrument modifier does not affect haste %. It only boosts secondary effects (STR/ATK/DS) — adds ~+2 avg damage/hit.
Confirmed — With Restriction
Composition of Ervaj stacks on McVaxius or VoV in its own unique haste slot (+8–9% swings/min). However: it does NOT stack with Elemental Rhythms or any Psalm song. This means you cannot run Ervaj whenever resist songs are needed. It is only viable for pure tank-and-spank fights (no meaningful AEs) — ideal for DPS groups and monk groups on trash or non-AE bosses.
Exception
McVaxius wins only if you're already at the haste cap from item haste + enchanter Celerity. At cap, VoV haste is wasted; McVaxius ATK/STR secondary becomes the tiebreaker.

Haste — Three Stacking Categories
Rule
Three categories stack additively: Item haste (belt) + Caster spell haste (enchanter) + Bard song haste. Within each category only highest applies. Instrument mod does NOT boost haste % on any song.

Drum barely boosts wind songs (Psalms) — near 1.0× on those. Puretone at 2.8× nearly doubles Psalm output. Epic at 1.8× gives Psalms mid-190s cold/fire (between drum and Puretone).
No Epic (Current)
Use Elemental Rhythms, not Psalms. Drum boosts Elemental (percussion = 157 fire / 160 cold). Psalms are wind songs — drum barely touches them (137/140). Psalms only win with epic or Puretone active.
With Epic
Swap to targeted Psalms. Epic 1.8× now makes Psalms beat Elemental for their specific element. Stack Guardian + Psalm of X, pop Puretone on the named pull to spike to 2.8×.
MR Stacking
Both Guardian Rhythms AND Elemental Rhythms contribute MR. That's why Guardian + Elemental hits 251 MR vs Guardian alone at 168. Swapping Elemental for a Psalm loses ~83 MR.

Dragon Necropolis — Zlandicar
Zone Overview
Primary Sleeper's Tomb key farming target. Zlandicar drops ST keys and is the main reason you're here. Belly caster — position group underneath him. Does NOT see invis — approach safely before the pull.
Tactical Note — MR Beats Disease Resist Here
Play Guardian + Elemental (max MR), not Purifying Rhythms. Stun Breath is a Magic 0 check — the more MR you stack, the more often you resist the 12-second stun. If the stun lands, ALL songs drop for 12 seconds and disease DoTs tick without Purifying anyway, plus the raid loses haste and Cantata. Resist the stun, keep your songs up, and let healers handle the disease DoTs. Playing disease resist and getting stunned gives you the worst of both worlds.

Sleeper's Tomb — Warders, Golems & the Sleeper
Zone Strategy — What You Actually Farm
Sleeper's Tomb has two halves. Upper zone — the four Warders: slowable dragons with cold/magic/fire breath AEs (resist fights). Golem room — the farm: Master of the Guard, The Progenitor, and The Final Arbiter. The golems cast no elemental breath AE — they're pure melee + add-wave fights, so a bard drops resist songs and runs the DPS/haste setup (VoV + McVaxius + Composition of Ervaj + Cantata). Each golem kill gives −35 Disciples of Kerafyrm faction and loot from the Primal Velium (pre-awakening) / Ancient Prismatic (post-awakening) weapon lines — and Composition of Ervaj itself drops in this zone. Exception: The Final Arbiter procs a cold DD on the tank (Frigid Shard Pummel) — its tank group runs cold, not Ervaj. Master of the Guard and The Progenitor have no confirmed tank DD.
⚠ Do NOT Wake the Sleeper
Killing all four Warders simultaneously wakes Kerafyrm — a one-time, irreversible, server-wide event that removes the Warders forever and unleashes an effectively unkillable dragon. Farm the golems; always leave at least one Warder alive. (Full Kerafyrm card at the bottom of this section.)
Bard Warning — Mesmerizing Breath
Ventani and Tukaarak both use Mesmerizing Breath — a magic silence that drops ALL your songs for 18 seconds (3 ticks). It is a targeted AE (not PB AE) — stay away from the tank to reduce exposure. Stack MR to resist it (Magic -150 check). All four warders must die before the Sleeper awakens.
